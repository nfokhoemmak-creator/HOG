Our story

Quiet luxury, at an honest price.

BAHRATI began with a simple frustration: accessories either looked beautiful and fell apart, or lasted forever and looked like hardware. We wanted both — pieces refined enough for a dinner table and tough enough for salt water.

So we build every piece from premium 316L stainless steel, seal it in thick 18K gold or sterling silver plating, and test it against sweat, sea and daily life. Then we back it with a 2-year warranty, because we know it will outlast the promise.
